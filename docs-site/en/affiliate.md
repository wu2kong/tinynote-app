# Affiliate and Partner Program

Last updated: August 23, 2026

If you create content about productivity tools, developer workflows, or local-first software, we welcome you to introduce TinyNote to your audience. We value honest experience and long-term trust over exaggerated promotion.

## Good ways to work together

- Product reviews, tutorials, and workflow walkthroughs
- Recommendations for developer, productivity, or local-first audiences
- Community events, co-created content, and product feedback exchanges

## Get in touch

Email [tinynote-app@wu2kong.com](mailto:tinynote-app@wu2kong.com) with your channel link, a short audience introduction, and your idea. We will reply with suitable collaboration options and the current arrangement when there is a fit.

## Promotion principles

Describe TinyNote’s features, pricing, and limits accurately. If content includes sponsorship, commission, or another material relationship, disclose it as required by your local rules and publishing platform. Do not use the TinyNote brand in misleading promotion or impersonate an official channel without written permission.
